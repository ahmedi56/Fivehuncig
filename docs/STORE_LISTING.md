**Full description:**

Not ready to quit? Start anyway.

You don't need a quit date. You don't need big willpower. FivehunCig starts where you are today.

😌 NOT READY? NO PROBLEM
• "Just watch" mode: tap each time you smoke. No pressure, no shame.
• When your numbers improve, FivehunCig offers the next small step.

⏱️ SMOKE LESS, SLOWLY
• Next Cigarette Timer: the app tells you when your next one is allowed.
• The wait grows a little each week: gentle, normal or faster.
• Cutting down first works as well as quitting all at once.

🆘 CRAVING SOS
• 5-minute delay timer: most cravings pass in 3 to 5 minutes.
• 1-minute breathing, Bubble pop, Memory match.
• Urge surfing: let the craving pass like a wave.

🧠 YOUR 30-SECOND PLAN
• "If I finish my coffee, then I take a short walk."
• Optional daily reminder at your hard moment.

❤️ STAY MOTIVATED
• Money saved and cigarettes not smoked.
• Your body's recovery timeline (based on WHO information).
• Badges for real progress, not for opening the app.
• Your reasons wall, with your own photos.
• Slipped? Nothing is reset. Restart in 2 minutes.

🔒 PRIVATE
• No account. No ads. Everything stays on your phone.

FivehunCig supports you. It does not replace a doctor. Ask a health professional about nicotine patches, gum or other medicines that can help.

Download FivehunCig. Start small. Break free.
