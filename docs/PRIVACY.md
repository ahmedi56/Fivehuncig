# FivehunCig privacy policy

_Last updated: 1 October 2026_

**Short version:** FivehunCig keeps everything on your phone. We don't collect, see or sell your data. The only exception is the optional AI chat, and only after you turn it on.

## What the app stores

On your phone only:

- The numbers you enter (cigarettes per day, pack price, awake hours).
- The times you log a cigarette or a craving, and, if you choose to tag it, what was behind it (for example "stress" or "coffee").
- Your language choice.
- Your urge-surfing check-ins (how strong the craving was, whether it helped), the Learn lessons you marked as read, and the weekly challenges you chose. Points, memories and "Your best" are calculated from these on your phone; nothing is shared or ranked against other people.
- Your If-Then plans and reasons, including any photos you choose to add.

## What we collect

Nothing, unless you turn on the AI chat. The app has no account, no analytics and no ads. Without the chat it does not use the internet except when you tap a help link, which opens in your browser.

## Optional AI chat

If you turn on "Talk it through":

- Your chat messages, and a few numbers (your approach, time since your last cigarette, how much you usually smoke, cravings you got through, and up to three common triggers) are sent through our server to Anthropic, which provides the AI (Claude), so it can reply.
- Your name, reasons, plans and photos are never sent.
- The app does not store the conversation. Our server keeps only technical counts (for example how long a reply took), never what you wrote.
- A random app id is sent to limit how many messages can be sent; it is not linked to you.
- You can turn the chat off in Settings at any time. "Delete all my data" also turns it off.

## Optional community

If you join the community:

- You get an anonymous account. We never ask for your email or phone number.
- Your nickname, the posts and replies you write, your reactions, saved posts, blocks and reports are stored on our server (Supabase), so other members can read posts and replies. Posts marked "Anonymous" never show who wrote them, to members or in the app.
- Your progress, cigarettes, cravings, plans, reasons and photos are never sent to the community.
- Moderators can see reported content and the nickname of its author, to keep the community safe.
- "Leave the community" or "Delete all my data" deletes everything you posted and your community profile.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** (optional): for "timer done" and your daily plan reminders. These are scheduled on your phone.
- **Photos** (optional): only when you choose to add a photo to your reasons. The photo is copied into the app's private storage.

## Your control

- **Export:** Settings → Export my data.
- **Delete:** Settings → Delete all my data, or uninstall the app.

## Health information

FivehunCig gives general support and information. It is not medical advice and does not replace a doctor.
